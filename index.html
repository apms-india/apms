Our Services

🪳 Cockroach Eliminator

Cockroaches enter your premises through cracks and drain pipes. Many diseases including allergies and asthma are caused through them. They remain active and keep multiplying all over the year. They are known for flying berserk everywhere, emitting unpleasant odors and making chirping noise, which can easily create havoc at your premises.

Treatment:

APMS’s service comprises of odourless chemical spray & gel treatment

🐭 Rodent Eliminator

Rodents have been known to damage buildings, contaminate food and also a major reason to cause fire by gnawing the cables of electrical wiring. They’re diverse; the rodent family includes rats, mice, bandicoot, gophers, and more. They’re also adaptable, extremely fertile (A pair can multiple by 800 to 1200 in a year); several dreaded disease including Lepidopterist are transmitted due to them. Thus controlling them is a real challenging task.

Treatment:

APMS’s service comprises of understanding & tracking the path of these rodents, thru our trained technician and then laying baits & traps accordingly. Periodic replenishment and relaying of baits would be monitored.

🐛 Termite Eliminator

Termites are known for damaging all kind of woods including the tables, shelves, storage doors, etc… They feed on cellulose for nutrition; and it is that cellulose is found in woods. The threats of infestation caused by termites are on higher note. They leave a "chemical trail" for others to follow. They only need a crack to gain entrance into your premise, once in, they can infest everything. Termites work 24 hours a day without even resting.

Treatment:

APMS’s service comprises drilling holes at skirting level/ junction of wall and floor, injecting them with herbal termiticide and sealing them. This makes a completely undetectable chemical barrier around your property. This treatment is always precautionary until & unless the entire building is treated for the infestation.

🛏️ Bedbug Eliminator

Bedbugs are often reported to be found when sanitation conditions are poor. They live feeding only on blood. They reside in tiny cracks in furniture as well as on textiles and upholstered furniture. They do not build nest, but live in huge groups. They tend to be most common on sofas, curtains; carpet’s and corner of wall papers. Bedbugs live for months without feeding, but lay hundreds of eggs in a short period.

Treatment:

APMS’s service comprises of spraying standard strong quality insecticides on the infected areas like sofas, Wall Cracks, Peeling of Color on wall Electric Switch Board bed, cushions etc. This treatment should preferably done when you know that the place can be kept shut for couple of hours. Infected linen or sofa cover should be washed in hot water.

🦟 Mosquito Eliminator

Mosquito being a blood eating pest, whose tube like mouth parts pierce the skin and cause an irritating rashes. The routine bites of mosquitos are extremely harmful and may transmit disease such as malaria, dengue fever, low blood pressure, etc. They lay eggs in stagnant water, plants, lakes, plastic buckets, flowerpot saucers and a biggest and serious nuisance to deal with.

Treatment:

APMS’s service comprises of fogging the periphery with standard quality insecticides and spray treatments where there is stagnant water, small plants in order to terminate mosquito larvae. This being the most recommended today.
